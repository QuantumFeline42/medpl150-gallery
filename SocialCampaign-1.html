Quantum Feline

Gallery Reflections Creative Statement

Social Campaign 1

For this project I want to explore the concept of neurodivergence and had the idea to use images of groups of people with people highlighted in various ways with secondary images that highlight various people and represent what is going in their minds and how their methods of thinking and processing are different but valuable in their own ways. I used the search tool at creativecommons.org to source images from different sites/search engines that have the CC license.

To accomplish this I used a black and photo for one of the images to allow the additional images to stand out, and for the second one I mostly desaturated the background aside from the selected people. For the first montage the space above their heads provided a good space to have the mental images extend upward and I used torn paper edges for the masking to give them a sense of being revealed. The images were also given a Hard Light blending mode to allow the background to show through and give them the feel of layering internal processes onto the world.
