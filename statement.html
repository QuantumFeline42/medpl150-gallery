Creative Statement

David Boyle is currently a Brooklyn-based graphic designer with over two decades of experience translating visual brands into physical spaces. Seeking to pivot into counseling and mediation by building on, rather than abandoning, their design background, David aims to combine their professional knowledge of how visuals convey information and their long-time interest in game design principles (e.g. structured systems, rules of engagement, and tactile representations) with their current focus on a degree in Psychology. Their eventual goal is to develop a unique approach to dealing with the differences between how every person’s unique brain interprets and reacts to information beyond simply verbal communication. By integrating graphic and game design concepts into their work they aim to better facilitate interpersonal communication and understanding.
